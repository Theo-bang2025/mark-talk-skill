# 更新记录 / Changelog

## 0.3.0 候选修订 / Candidate refinement · 2026-10-30

接收一位参与者的全部10组反馈，保留候选3组获选、旧版5组、并列2组及原客观失败记录。此次小范围修补增加“承担任务＋准确限制＋可行渠道”的组织方式；批评回应以具体修正表达态度，未接受的更大工作或日期先给用户选择。紧急求助无需列举未做的排查；未完成的状态不扩写成完全没动。明确“只给最终一条”的输出形态，以及普通拟稿/表达偏好不授权文件、记忆和索引写入。

版本字段保留0.3.0，新增修订日期区分旧冻结包；没有覆盖原测评或创建正式发布标签。反馈、定向复测的实际完成量与限制见[本次报告](tests/reports/2026-10-29-0.3.0-human-feedback-and-refinement.md)。

Received ten paired judgments from one participant: candidate preferred in three, old in five, two ties. Targeted changes connect responsibility, precise constraints and a feasible channel; respond to criticism with concrete corrections; keep new work or dates subject to user acceptance; prioritize urgent help; and return only the requested final wording. Ordinary drafting and preference corrections do not authorize persistence. Version remains 0.3.0 with a revision date. Historical failures remain; this is not formal acceptance or a release tag.

## 0.3.0 候选 / Candidate · 2026-10-21

新增随包小型向上沟通方法库，覆盖进展、求助、建议、标准、资源、约束、批评和确认。核心按当前任务选方法，必要时追问，补充后更新，选稿后默认只改一条。方法用于组织思路，最终表达按关系、媒介和个人习惯变化，允许省略和调序；润色不得改变事实、立场、约束或承诺。

细化已做行动、时间交付、可用性/小额外工作和让步检查；不做笼统“全部无承诺”认证。整卡停用后也不再引用旧事项邀请下一任务；明确文本作废与指定存储副本的不同入口。

新增六个迁移/多轮任务、反向可用性与自然表达诊断；修订评分漏判、记忆/工具证据和人类体验记录。默认验收为0.2.0/候选比较，预定29新会话40响应及10次人类任务反馈。双语教程展示补充→选稿→改口吻。开发结果和仍待取得的真实宿主/人类证据见[候选报告](tests/reports/2026-10-21-0.3.0-candidate.md)。尚未创建正式发布标签或声称千问候选验收通过。

Added eight bundled upward-communication methods, selective clarification, fact-aware revisions and convergence after selection. Methods guide thinking; messages follow relationship, medium and the user's voice. Polishing preserves facts, position, constraints and commitments. Checks now distinguish completed actions, delivery, availability/extra effort and concessions. Withdrawn cards no longer supply old matters for follow-up offers; text withdrawal and specifically requested storage operations are separate.

Added transfer/dialogue scripts and diagnostics, updated evidence/measurement guidance and bilingual worked tutorials. The planned old/candidate campaign is 29 conversations / 40 responses plus ten human task evaluations. Developer replay, actual host loading and human preference are reported separately. This is a candidate, without a formal release tag or claimed Qianwen acceptance.

## 0.2.0 · 2026-10-19

复核首轮千问用户内测后，重点修订事实与承诺边界。正文中的部分交付、短时完成和资源让步，同样需要用户确认；未知称呼默认省略。关系卡继续作为用户控制的文本，普通操作不主动检索工作区或宿主记忆，动作声明按实际证据限定范围。

新增中英文介绍、上手演示、指导 AI 与人类共用的内测流程。质量评分与产品格式约定分开；两组都在 S8 后追加 S9-b，明确十八个新会话和二十次响应。首轮高分不再作为有效性结论，新修订仍需千问客户端复测。

After reviewing the first participant-run Qianwen trial, this revision tightens the treatment of unconfirmed facts, partial deliveries, short-duration promises, and resource concessions. Unknown names are omitted. Cards remain user-controlled text; ordinary card tasks do not initiate workspace or host-memory searches, and action claims must match the evidence available.

Added Chinese and English introductions, worked examples, and an evaluation procedure for human participants and AI coaches. Quality is scored separately from product format. Both conditions now append S9-b to S8: eighteen new conversations and twenty responses. The earlier high scores are not retained as an effectiveness claim. The revised skill still needs a repeat test in the Qianwen client.

# 更新记录 / Changelog

## 0.2.0 · 2026-10-19

复核首轮千问用户内测后，重点修订事实与承诺边界。正文中的部分交付、短时完成和资源让步，同样需要用户确认；未知称呼默认省略。关系卡继续作为用户控制的文本，普通操作不主动检索工作区或宿主记忆，动作声明按实际证据限定范围。

新增中英文介绍、上手演示、指导 AI 与人类共用的内测流程。质量评分与产品格式约定分开；两组都在 S8 后追加 S9-b，明确十八个新会话和二十次响应。首轮高分不再作为有效性结论，新修订仍需千问客户端复测。

After reviewing the first participant-run Qianwen trial, this revision tightens the treatment of unconfirmed facts, partial deliveries, short-duration promises, and resource concessions. Unknown names are omitted. Cards remain user-controlled text; ordinary card tasks do not initiate workspace or host-memory searches, and action claims must match the evidence available.

Added Chinese and English introductions, worked examples, and an evaluation procedure for human participants and AI coaches. Quality is scored separately from product format. Both conditions now append S9-b to S8: eighteen new conversations and twenty responses. The earlier high scores are not retained as an effectiveness claim. The revised skill still needs a repeat test in the Qianwen client.
